# Data

The raw datasets are not committed to this repository. Download them from their original providers and place them here:

```text
data/
├── raw/
│   ├── exchange_rate.csv
│   └── wfp_food_prices_som.csv
└── processed/
    └── generated alert and summary CSV files
```

Sources:

- [WFP Somalia Food Prices](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/wfp-food-prices-for-somalia)
- [World Bank Microdata Library](https://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/6155/get-microdata)

Check the providers' current licenses and terms before redistributing their data.
